TOOLS Four apps

Four apps that came before the map, not out of it.

Each one came from noticing a gap directly — something being measured badly, or not at all — and building the thing that would measure it. That happened before the decomposition was anywhere near able to point at it.

They are independent tools, not a suite. They share no data and no account, and each keeps its own name, colour and identity.

None of them is placed on the map. All four are about the optimisation of learning and attention, which means each touches many parts of many systems at once rather than sitting inside one of them, and the decomposition has not reached the node level in any domain. Any attribution offered now would be invented.
