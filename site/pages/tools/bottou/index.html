Bottou 没頭

Free on the App Store · iPhone, Mac

There is no pause button.

You tap start when you drop into work, and stop the moment your attention breaks. A distraction does not pause the block — it ends it. A pause would let the break hide inside the measurement.

Not derived from the map · built from a gap noticed directly

Download on the App Store

§ 01 The loop

Start, stop, and two taps to close it out.

Nothing is measured without your tap, and nothing runs in the background. The timer is a sensor you operate deliberately.

Start

One tap. One large clock, counting up. No countdown, no presets, nothing to configure before you begin.

Stop

The moment focus breaks, that block is over. If you sit back down afterwards, that is a new block rather than a continuation of this one.

Close it out

Two taps: why you stopped, and how the focus felt. Anything under a minute is discarded as a misclick.

§ 02 The two taps

The reason a block ends is data too.

Four answers — done, distracted, interrupted, ran out of steam. Over a month the distribution tells you whether you are finishing your work or being finished by something else.

The focus scale is anchored to attention rather than output. A short block you were locked into scores higher than a long one you kept dragging yourself back to. Five is losing track of time; one is barely holding it together.

§ 03 Progress

The timer is the sensor. The picture is the product.

There is no tree to grow and nothing to win today. Every figure is shown against the previous equal period, because an average on its own means nothing.

The weekday-against-time-of-day view is the question the app exists to answer: when do you actually focus well? You cannot answer that from memory, and you cannot answer it from a total.

Focus

The clock and one button. It reads ready when idle and focusing when it is running. That is the whole screen.

Calendar

A month grid, each day marked in its average focus colour. Drill into any day to see its blocks, and write a reflection for today or a past day.

Progress

Averages against the previous equal period, a breakdown of why you stop, a day-by-day chart, and the weekday heatmap, over seven days, thirty days, or all of it.

Settings

Account, lifetime totals, about, reset. An account is optional and exists only to sync between your iPhone and your Mac.

§ 04 Deliberately absent

A mirror for your focus, not a monitor.

Nothing is automatic and nothing is scored against you. The numbers are only as honest as your taps, and the app does not pretend otherwise.

  • No pause buttonA break ends the block. That is the measurement.
  • No countdown or presetsThe clock counts up from zero.
  • No leaderboards, friends or feedThere is nobody else in the app.
  • No trees to growAnd no streaks to protect.
  • No automatic trackingNothing is measured without your tap.
  • No advertisingNot now and not later. Systems Atlas does not do advertising.

§ 05 On the name

没頭

Bottou — total absorption. 没 is to sink or submerge; 頭 is the head. The state of being so absorbed you lose track of time. This app measures how often you reach it.

The Japanese naming is not decoration. English lacks single unclaimed words for these compressed ideas.
