Konki 根気

Free on the App Store · iPhone, iPad, Mac

A training app that counts what you actually did.

Calisthenics, weights and cardio in one log. Every target is derived from your own history rather than a template written in advance, and a pull-up is not recorded as zero kilos.

Not derived from the map · built from a gap noticed directly

Download on the App Store

§ 01 Three modalities

One log, whatever the session was.

Konki started as a calisthenics app. It is not one any more, but the part that made it worth using has not been diluted to make room for the rest.

Bodyweight, counted properly

Bodyweight movements carry your own mass as load. A set of pull-ups is scored against what you actually moved, so bodyweight work and loaded work can sit in the same volume figure without one of them reading as nothing.

Estimated maxes you can argue with

A one-rep max is estimated from your working sets, adjusted for the reps you had left in reserve. The estimate is shown as an estimate, with the sets it came from, so you can disagree with it on evidence.

Paces derived from real efforts

Cardio targets come from your own best recent efforts rather than from an age-based formula. Miss a stretch of training and the targets fall, because that is what the evidence says.

189 exercises, and your own

The catalogue covers 189 exercises across 74 base movements, and you can add your own. If a name matches a known movement, Konki offers to link it so it inherits the muscle attribution. If not, you pick one primary muscle — it will not invent a percentage split it cannot support.

You can also skip tagging altogether. An untagged exercise logs and charts normally, stays out of volume and strain analysis, and says so rather than quietly distorting the totals.

§ 02 Training with someone

A coach can send you a programme. That is the whole of it.

Connect with a friend or a coach by username. They can send you templates and multi-week programmes, and message you. A coach sees the programmes shared between you and the sessions logged against them — not your other training, not your body metrics, not anything you have not deliberately sent.

Sharing your training log is separate again, and off unless you turn it on. You can block any account from any conversation and report any message. Reports are read by a person within 24 hours. There is no feed and no follower count, and there will not be one.

§ 03 Constraints

What Konki will not do.

Each of these makes a number less comfortable and more true. That trade is the design, not a feature that has not been built yet.

  • No advertisingNot now and not later, and not a tactic held in reserve while the app is free.
  • No invented numbersIf a figure cannot be derived from what you logged, it is not shown.
  • No rewriting historyPast sessions stay as they were recorded.
  • No account requiredThe app works fully offline and on device.
  • No borrowed programmesEverything bundled was written for Konki.
  • No growth into a platformIt measures training. It is not becoming something else.

§ 04 On the name

根気

Konki — the stubbornness that finishes things. Not motivation, which burns off. Not willpower, which is a thing you run out of. The low, steady kind of persistence that shows up again on a day when nothing about it is interesting.
