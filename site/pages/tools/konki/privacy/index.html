KONKI Privacy policy

What Konki collects, and what it does not.

Last updated 25 July 2026

Konki is built by Systems Atlas Project. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights over it.

Konki works fully without an account. Everything below applies only if you choose to create one.

What is collected

When you create an account:

  • Your email address
  • A hashed version of your password — the password itself is never stored
  • Your training data: workout logs, exercise plans, set and rep records

If you use Konki's social features, also:

  • A username, if you choose one. This is public to other Konki users — it is how people find you, and anyone can search for it.
  • Your connections — who you have added as a friend or coach, and who has added you.
  • Messages you send to and receive from your connections, stored as written.
  • Templates and programmes you share, including anything you have written in them.
  • Blocks — a record of accounts you have blocked.
  • Reports you file, including a copy of the content you reported, taken at the moment you report it.
  • Log sharing consent — a record of which coaches you have chosen to share your training log with.

No analytics, crash reports, location data, or any other personal information is collected.

How your data is used

Solely to provide the Konki service:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To sync your training data across your devices
  • To deliver messages and shared templates between you and your connections
  • To operate blocking, so a blocked account can no longer find or contact you
  • To review reports and act on accounts that break the rules
  • To send password reset emails when you request them

Your data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

What other people can see

Your training data is private. Nobody else can see your logs, plans or body metrics unless you deliberately share a template or programme with them.

Other Konki users can see your username if you have set one, messages you send them, and templates and programmes you share with them. If you connect a coach, they can see the templates and programmes you share and the sessions logged against them.

Sharing your training log is separate, and off unless you turn it on.

Accepting a coach does not give them access to your calendar. If you choose to share your log with a specific coach, they can see every session in it — including sessions from before you connected — with the exercises, sets, durations and notes you recorded. Your body metrics, your goals, and anything not logged as a session stay private either way.

You can stop sharing at any time, from Connections. Access ends immediately.

Blocking and reports

Blocking is silent. The blocked account is not told. From their side you stop appearing in search, they cannot message you, and your connection ends.

When you report someone, the report and a copy of the reported content are stored. This is because reported content can be deleted or changed before anyone reads the report, and without a copy there may be nothing left to review.

A person reads every report within 24 hours. Accounts that break the rules in the Terms of Use are removed.

Reports are not anonymous to me — I can see who filed them. They are never shown to the person reported.

Where your data is stored

On servers hosted by Railway in the European Union. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Authentication tokens are signed as JSON Web Tokens.

Email

Resend is used to send transactional email — specifically password resets. There are no marketing emails and no mailing list. Your email address is not shared with Resend beyond what is necessary to deliver those messages.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP address in order to deliver the page and protect against abuse.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from inside the app: Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, your training data, your connections, and your block list. You can also request deletion by email.

Two things survive that deletion, and it is worth being straight about them:

  • Messages you sent to other people remain in their conversations. Your side of someone else's chat history cannot be removed without deleting theirs too.
  • Reports filed about your account are kept for up to 12 months. Otherwise deleting an account would erase the record of why it was reported, and someone could clear their history by signing up again.

Reports you filed about other people are kept for the same period, for the same reason.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data held about you, request correction of anything inaccurate, request deletion, and export your data. To exercise any of these, get in touch through the support page.

Children

Konki is not directed at children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page is updated. Continued use of the app after that constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
