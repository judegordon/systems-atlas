Common questions

Why did my streak reset?

A run counts consecutive scheduled days. If a rule was due on a day and you marked it broken, the run goes to zero and starts again from the next kept day. There are no freezes, repairs or grace days — that is the point of the app rather than an oversight.

Days a rule was not scheduled for do not break the run. If a rule only runs on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, the days between are ignored entirely.

Today shows as incomplete. Have I already lost the streak?

No. Today is not counted as missed until you review it. Kantetsu is retrospective: you look back at a day once it is over. A day you have not logged is simply unreviewed.

Why can't I change a rule's schedule for past days?

Once a rule has been logged, its schedule is locked for the days already behind you, and any change applies from now on. If schedules could be rewritten backwards, every past streak would become editable and the number would stop meaning anything. Rule titles have no effect on the arithmetic and stay editable, so typos are always fixable.

How do I get my history onto another device?

Create an account in the app, then upload from the device that has your history and download onto the other one. Sync is manual and directional — you choose which way it goes, and nothing is merged silently behind you.

I archived a rule. Is its history gone?

No. Archived rules keep everything. You can reveal them at any time to look back at what they recorded.

Will there be notifications?

No. Kantetsu does not nag, and adding reminders would make it a different app. Reviewing yesterday is a habit you keep or do not — which is the thing being measured.

How do I delete my account or data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Account deletion is available in the app and permanently removes your account and everything uploaded with it from the server. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

Is Kantetsu free?

Yes. Free to download, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertising.
