KANTETSU Privacy policy

What Kantetsu stores, and what it does not.

Last updated 30 July 2026

Kantetsu keeps your rules, your daily marks and your notes on your own device. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. An account is optional and exists only so your history can move between your own devices.

Data stored on your device

Used without an account, everything stays on your device and is included in your own device backups:

  • The rules you write, and their schedules
  • Your daily kept-or-broken marks
  • Your daily reflection notes
  • App settings

None of this leaves your device unless you create an account and choose to upload it.

If you create an account

An account is entirely optional and the app is fully usable without one.

Creating an account stores your email address and a hashed password. Sync is manual and directional — you choose to upload your history or download it, and nothing is transferred without you asking for it. Uploaded data is the same data listed above, held on servers operated for the Systems Atlas Project and used for one purpose: giving it back to you on your other devices.

Your email address identifies your account and is used to send password resets. It is not used for marketing, and there is no mailing list.

What Kantetsu does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data
  • No profiling, and no use of your data to train anything
  • No access to contacts, photos, location or health data

Where your data is stored

On servers hosted by Railway in the European Union. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Authentication tokens are signed as JSON Web Tokens.

Email

Resend is used to send transactional email — specifically password resets. There are no marketing emails and no mailing list. Your email address is not shared with Resend beyond what is necessary to deliver those messages.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP address in order to deliver the page and protect against abuse.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. If you have an account, you can delete it from within the app, which permanently removes your account and all data uploaded with it from the server. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data held about you, request correction of anything inaccurate, request deletion, and export your data. To exercise any of these, get in touch through the support page.

Children

Kantetsu is not directed at children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page is updated. Continued use of the app after that constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: kantetsu@systemsatlasproject.com.
