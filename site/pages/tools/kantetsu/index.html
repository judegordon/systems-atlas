§ 01 The loop

Three things happen, and nothing else does.

Nothing is measured while the day is happening. There is no sensor, no timer and no background process — you are the only source, which is the point rather than a limitation.

Write the rules

Your commitments, in your own words. Each one runs every day, or only on the days you choose.

Look back at yesterday

Mark each rule kept or broken. Because nothing was recorded while you lived the day, the mark is a judgement you make rather than a number the app produces.

Leave one note

140 characters on how the day felt. Not a journal — a margin note short enough that you will actually reread it.

§ 02 Strict by design

Most habit apps forgive a missed day. This one does not.

A kept run counts consecutive scheduled days. Miss a day you were due, and the run goes to zero. No freezes, no repairs, no grace period, and nothing bought back with points.

That is what the name means. A streak that forgives you is not measuring anything.

Today stays blank until you log it. It is not counted as missed — it simply has not been reviewed yet.

§ 03 In the app

Enough to be honest with yourself, and no more.

Rules and schedules

A rule runs every day, or only on the days you pick — exercise on Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday. Each rule tracks its own current streak, best streak and adherence rate.

Schedules that lock

Once a rule has been logged, its schedule locks going forward. Change it and the change applies from today on; past streaks are never retroactively rewritten. Titles stay editable, so typos are fixable. The arithmetic stays honest.

Calendar

Every day at a glance: kept, broken, or not yet reviewed. Filter to a single rule to see how you have held one specific commitment over time.

Insights

Per-rule kept rates over the last 30 days and all time, weekly kept-rate bars, and an app-wide chart of what fraction of your rules you kept each day with a weekly average drawn over it.

Archive

Retire a rule and it keeps its history. Reveal archived rules whenever you want to look back at what they recorded.

Local-first, with optional sync

The app works entirely without an account. If you want your history on another device, an account carries it — a manual upload or download, your choice of direction, never a silent merge.

§ 04 Deliberately absent

What a quiet app leaves out.

  • No notificationsIt never nags. Reviewing yesterday is itself a habit you keep or do not.
  • No live trackingNothing runs in the background.
  • No points, badges or levelsThe number is the adherence rate. Nothing decorates it.
  • No streak freezes or repairsA broken run is a broken run.
  • No feed, friends or leaderboardThere is nobody else in the app.
  • No advertisingNot now and not later. Systems Atlas does not do advertising.

§ 05 On the name

貫徹

Kantetsu — resolve carried through to the end. 貫 is to pierce or carry through; 徹 is to penetrate thoroughly. Together they mean not stopping until it is done.

The Japanese naming is not decoration. English lacks single unclaimed words for these compressed ideas.
