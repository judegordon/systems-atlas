SHUTOKU Privacy policy

What Shutoku stores, and what it does not.

Last updated 30 July 2026

Shutoku is built by Systems Atlas Project. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights over it.

Shutoku works fully without an account. Everything below applies only if you choose to create one.

What is collected

  • Your email address
  • A hashed version of your password — the password itself is never stored
  • Your vocabulary and review data: words you have added, review schedules, and progress records

No analytics, no crash reporting, no location data, and no advertising identifiers.

How your data is used

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To sync your vocabulary and review data across your devices
  • To send password reset emails when you request them

Your data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising or to train anything.

Where your data is stored

On servers hosted by Railway in the European Union. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Authentication tokens are signed as JSON Web Tokens.

Email

Resend is used to send transactional email — specifically password resets. There are no marketing emails and no mailing list. Your email address is not shared with Resend beyond what is necessary to deliver those messages.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP address in order to deliver the page and protect against abuse.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all data uploaded with it from the server, and clears the local data on the device. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Deleting the app without deleting the account removes the local copy only.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data held about you, request correction of anything inaccurate, request deletion, and export your data. To exercise any of these, get in touch through the support page.

Children

Shutoku is not directed at children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page is updated. Continued use of the app after that constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: shutoku@systemsatlasproject.com.
