Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Shutoku works fully without one. An account exists only to sync your vocabulary and review data between your own devices.

How does the spaced repetition work?

Shutoku tracks how securely you know each word and schedules its next review for the point just before recall is likely to fail. Words you know well appear less often; words you keep losing come back sooner. There is nothing to configure.

What levels does it cover?

Vocabulary across all JLPT levels, N5 through N1. You can filter the dictionary by level and focus reviews on the band you are actually working at.

How does conjugation practice work?

The conjugation engine covers て-form, た-form, negative, potential, causative and passive. Any verb in the dictionary can be practised in any of those forms.

Can I use romaji instead of kana?

Yes, and you can switch at any time between hiragana, katakana and romaji. Reducing romaji as your reading improves is the intended path, but the app will not force it.

How do I sync between devices?

Create an account in Settings → Account. Once signed in, your vocabulary and review schedule sync across your devices.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email address. A reset link arrives within a few minutes. Check spam if it does not.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything uploaded with it from the server, and clears the local data on the device. It cannot be undone.

Is Shutoku free?

Yes. Free to download, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertising.
